Chapter 1

BEFORE AN EARTHQUAKE: PREPARATION

Learn how 4th-grade students in SJL prepare before an earthquake happens.

Grammar: SHOULD / SHOULDN'T

Emergency Backpack (Mochila de Emergencia)

Always keep water, first aid supplies, and a flashlight ready at home and school.

Reading: Preparation in San Juan de Lurigancho

Earthquakes can happen at any moment in Lima. Students at I.E. N.° 0148 Maestro Víctor Raúl Haya de la Torre learn how to prepare before an emergency.

First, every family should prepare an emergency kit with water, non-perishable food, a flashlight, and a first aid kit. Second, students should identify the safe area in their classroom and know the evacuation route.

We shouldn’t block doors or hallways with heavy backpacks. Also, families should organize a clear family plan. Finally, all students should participate responsibly in every school drill!

Chapter 2

DURING AN EARTHQUAKE: ACTION

How to act calmly inside the school classroom during seismic movement.

Grammar: SHOULD / SHOULDN'T

Drop, Cover & Hold On

Protect your head under desks and stay away from glass windows.

Reading: Seismic Safety in the Classroom

It is Tuesday morning at our school in San Juan de Lurigancho. Suddenly, the ground begins to shake!

Students must stay calm immediately. You should protect your head and neck with your hands or cover under a heavy desk.

During the shaking, students shouldn’t run, shouldn’t push, and shouldn’t shout. Stay away from glass windows and listen carefully to the teacher’s instructions. Do not use stairs while the floor is shaking!

Chapter 3

AFTER AN EARTHQUAKE: EVACUATION

Following evacuation protocols and building safety post-earthquake.

Grammar: FIRST CONDITIONAL

Safe Meeting Point (Punto de Encuentro)

Walk calmly to the designated safety circle in the school yard.

Reading: Safe Evacuation Protocol

When the tremor stops, after the earthquake, students wait for the teacher’s signal.

Everyone should follow the official evacuation route calmly. Walk in an orderly line to the school courtyard’s safe meeting point (marked with a green "S" circle).

Stay with your class group so teachers can check attendance. If someone needs help, inform an adult immediately. If we follow the safety rules, we will be safer. Never return to the classroom without authorization!

Grammar Corner

LET'S LEARN GRAMMAR!

Key grammar structures for Unit 7: Recommendations and Conditional Actions.

A. SHOULD

Advice & Recommendations

Subject + should + base verb
  • We should stay calm.
  • Students should follow the plan.
  • You should prepare an emergency kit.

B. SHOULDN'T

Actions Not Recommended

Subject + shouldn't + base verb
  • We shouldn't run during shaking.
  • Students shouldn't push classmates.
  • You shouldn't stand near windows.

C. FIRST CONDITIONAL

Real Possibility & Consequence

If + present simple, will + base verb
  • If we follow rules, we will be safer.
  • If students run, they will put themselves at risk.
  • If we prepare, we will be ready.
